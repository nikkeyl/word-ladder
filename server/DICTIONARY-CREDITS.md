# Dictionary credits

- Russian common nouns were extracted from OpenCorpora and filtered to single
  words. The data is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
  [Dictionary distribution](https://github.com/no-plagiarism/pymorphy3-dicts) ·
  [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- Japanese noun readings were extracted from JMdict by Jim Breen and the
  Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), normalized
  to hiragana and filtered to single kana words. The data is licensed under
  CC BY-SA 4.0. [JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/JMdict-EDICT_Dictionary_Project) ·
  [EDRDG license](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html)
- English nouns were extracted from Princeton WordNet 3.1 and filtered to
  single lowercase ASCII words. The original notice is in
  `WORDNET-LICENSE.txt`. [Princeton WordNet](https://wordnet.princeton.edu/)
